About Us

We're Brebotics, the first ever robotics team at Brébeuf, born from passionate students that want to take our passion for Arduinos, circuits and programming even further.
We've split ourselves into 6 teams: comms, video, competitive programming, robot hardware, robot software, and partnerships. Together, we're making our robot, an F1-meets-cyberpunk machine which we affectionately refer to as Brebot, a reality.
The Competition
We're supported by the Physics department at Brébeuf, our Mathematics & Computer Science department and our student association. If you're from industry and would like to partner with us, contact us via team [at] crcbrebeuf.ca.
